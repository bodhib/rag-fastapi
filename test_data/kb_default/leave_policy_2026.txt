ACME INDIA — EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY
Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: People Operations

Annual Leave
Employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave should normally be requested at least three working days in advance through the HR portal.

Carry Forward
Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual-leave days into the following calendar year. Any balance above 5 days expires at the end of the calendar year unless an exception is approved in writing by People Operations.

Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave of more than two consecutive working days may require a medical certificate.

Approval
Annual leave requires manager approval before the leave begins. Managers should consider team coverage and business-critical deadlines when approving requests.

Public Holidays
Company-declared public holidays are not deducted from annual-leave balances.
