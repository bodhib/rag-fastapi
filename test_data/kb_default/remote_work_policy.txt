ACME INDIA — REMOTE WORK POLICY
Effective date: 1 February 2026

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their role is suitable for remote work. Employees must remain reachable during their team's agreed working hours and must use company-approved devices and security controls.

Remote work is not a substitute for approved leave. Employees who are unavailable because of illness must follow the sick-leave process.

Teams may designate specific collaboration days when employees are expected to attend the office.
