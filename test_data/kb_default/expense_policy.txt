ACME INDIA — BUSINESS EXPENSE POLICY
Effective date: 1 March 2026

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses that are necessary for approved company activities.

Receipts
Original or digital receipts should be submitted with an expense claim. Claims without supporting documentation may be rejected.

Submission deadline
Expense claims should normally be submitted within 30 calendar days after the expense is incurred.

Travel
Economy-class air travel is the standard for domestic trips. Hotel bookings should use the company's approved travel process unless an exception is approved.
