ACME INDIA — CUSTOMER SUPPORT SERVICE LEVELS
Version: 2.0

Priority P1
A complete service outage affecting multiple customers is treated as P1. The support team should acknowledge a P1 ticket within 15 minutes and provide updates at least every 30 minutes until service is restored.

Priority P2
A major degradation without a complete outage is treated as P2. Target acknowledgement is one hour.

Priority P3
Routine questions and minor issues are treated as P3. Target acknowledgement is four business hours.
