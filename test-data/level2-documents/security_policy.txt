ACME Technologies
Information Security Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Employees must use strong passwords and must not share passwords with colleagues.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems that support MFA.

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption.

Employees must immediately report suspected phishing emails, credential theft, lost company devices, or unauthorized access to the Security Operations Team.

Confidential company information must not be uploaded to personal cloud storage or sent to personal email accounts.

Security incidents should be reported within 24 hours of discovery.

Employees working remotely must use the approved corporate VPN when accessing internal systems.
