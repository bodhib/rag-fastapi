ACME Technologies
Employee Leave Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Annual Leave

All full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year.

Carry-forward leave must be used by March 31 of the following year. Any unused carried-forward leave after March 31 will expire.

Employees should submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 3 working days before the planned leave date.

For emergency leave, employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Medical documentation may be requested for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive working days.

Public Holidays

Employees are entitled to company-declared public holidays according to the annual holiday calendar.

Probation

Employees on probation receive the same annual leave entitlement, but leave approval remains subject to manager approval and business requirements.
