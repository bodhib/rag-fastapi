EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Acme Technologies
Effective January 2026

1. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days into
the following calendar year.

Any unused annual leave above the 5-day carry-forward limit expires
at the end of the calendar year.

Employees should submit annual leave requests through the HR portal
at least 3 working days before the intended leave date.

Emergency leave may be requested with shorter notice and must be
approved by the employee's manager.

2. Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

Remote work is subject to manager approval and business requirements.

Employees working remotely must remain available during their
normal working hours.

3. Parental Leave

Eligible employees may take up to 16 weeks of paid parental leave.

Parental leave should normally be requested at least 30 days before
the expected start date where reasonably possible.

4. Probation

New employees normally have a 6-month probation period.

The probation period may be extended by up to 3 months when additional
performance assessment is required.

5. Expenses

Business travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the
expense date.

Receipts are required for individual expenses above INR 1,000.

6. Learning and Development

Employees may claim up to INR 25,000 per financial year for approved
professional training and certification.

Training must be approved by the employee's manager before enrollment.
