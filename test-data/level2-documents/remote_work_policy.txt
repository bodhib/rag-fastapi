ACME Technologies
Remote Work Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.

Employees must work from a location with a reliable internet connection and maintain normal working hours.

Employees handling confidential customer information must use the company VPN when working outside the office.

Remote employees are expected to attend mandatory meetings and remain reachable through approved communication tools.

New employees may become eligible for remote work after completing their first 30 calendar days of employment.

Managers may require employees to work from the office temporarily when business requirements, client meetings, training, or team activities require physical presence.
