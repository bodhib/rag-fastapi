ACME Technologies
Business Expense Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Employees may claim reimbursement for reasonable business expenses incurred while performing approved company activities.

Travel expenses must be supported by valid receipts.

Hotel accommodation is reimbursable up to INR 5,000 per night unless a higher limit has been approved in advance.

Domestic air travel should normally be booked in economy class.

Employees must submit expense claims within 30 calendar days of the expense date.

Expenses above INR 25,000 require prior approval from the employee's department head.

Personal expenses, entertainment expenses without business justification, and expenses without required documentation may be rejected.
